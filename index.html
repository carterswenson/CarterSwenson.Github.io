About

I'm a Planetary Science graduate student with interests spanning observational astronomy, atmospheric science, computational modeling, surface processes and scientific visualization. My work focuses on understanding complex physical systems through data analysis, mathematical modeling, and simulation.

Research Interests

Brown Dwarfs

Atmospheric properties, stellar evolution, detection methods, and cloud modeling.

Exoplanets and Atmospheres

Detection methods, atmospheric characterization and evolution, planet formation, and habitability.

Surface Processes

Studying Crater Rays and Lunar cold spots, as well as surface processes on icy moons like Europa.

Featured Work

Atmospheric Modeling

Using 1D atmospheric models to explore the behavior of planetary atmospheres.

Surface cratering on rocky bodies

Studying the surface features formed by impact craters on planets and moons and connecting their formation mechanisms.

Surface weathering on Icy Moons

Conducting experiments simulating the surface processes on moons like Europa and Charon.
